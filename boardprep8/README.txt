BOARDPREP 100 — CLASS 8 EDITION
================================

RUN THE APP
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted boardprep8 folder.
3. Double-click index.html, or open it in Edge/Chrome.
4. The dashboard, notes, practice answers, planner, mistake book and timer work offline.
5. An internet connection is needed for embedded NCERT solution pages.

SUBJECTS INCLUDED — ONLY THE FIVE MAIN SUBJECTS
- Mathematics — Ganita Prakash, Part I and Part II (14 chapters)
- Science — Curiosity (13 chapters)
- Social Science — Exploring Society: India and Beyond, Part I and Part II (15 chapters)
- English — Poorvi (5 units containing 15 individual lessons/poems)
- Hindi — Malhar (10 chapters)

CHAPTER WORKSPACE
- “Write Your Own Notes” replaces the old Notes label.
- NCERT Solutions are loaded inside the chapter workspace using an embedded page. The app does not intentionally open a separate browser tab. External solution providers may block embedded viewing through their own security settings; if that happens, the embedded area may remain blank.
- “15 Practice Questions” provides 15 original, subject-aware written revision prompts for every chapter/lesson. These are additional practice prompts, not verbatim NCERT exercise questions.
- Answers to the 15 practice prompts can be saved locally for each chapter.
- There is no Question Bank tab.
- Chapter completion tracking, study planner, mistake book, dashboard and focus timer are retained.

CONTENT AND EDITION NOTES
The chapter catalogue follows the NCERT Class 8 2026–27 books: Ganita Prakash Parts I and II, Curiosity, Exploring Society Parts I and II, Poorvi and Malhar. English is listed as its 15 individual textbook lessons under the five Poorvi units.

Solution references used for in-app embedded viewing include Teachoo (Mathematics, Science and English), ClearStudy (Social Science) and Saraswat Academy (Hindi). These are third-party references, not NCERT-authored publications. Their availability and ability to display inside an embedded frame are controlled by those providers.

LOCAL STORAGE
Notes, saved practice answers, chapter completion, tasks, mistakes, scores, focus sessions and points are stored in this browser on this device. No cloud database is used. Clearing browser site data can erase local progress. Existing Class 8 data using boardprep_class8_data_v1 is retained where the same chapter keys still match.

FOCUS TIMER & POINTS
- One completed 25-minute focus session = 50 points.
- Points are awarded only after the full session finishes.
- Timer state and completed sessions are saved in LocalStorage.
- Refreshing or reopening the app restores an active or paused timer.
- Resetting an unfinished session does not award points.
